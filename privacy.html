LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

At The Horizoon ("we," "our," or "us"), we value your trust above all else. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect, how that information is utilized, and the robust technical safeguards we enforce to ensure your digital autonomy remains protected.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in radical data minimization. We only collect information strictly required to deliver high-quality editorial content and interactive experiences:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: When you subscribe to our Sunday briefing, we collect your email address solely to transmit our editorial digests.
  • Reader Inquiries & Comments: When you submit our contact form or leave a comment on an article, we collect your submitted name, email address, and message text.
  • Local Preferences (Client-Side Only): Bookmarked articles, saved stories, and local reading progress are stored strictly on your device's browser via localStorage and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data exclusively for:

  • Delivering requested weekly editorial newsletters.
  • Responding directly to customer service and editorial story pitches.
  • Monitoring site performance and preventing security intrusions.

We will never sell, rent, monetize, or barter your email address or personal details to third-party data brokers or advertising exchanges.

3. Cookies and Analytics

The Horizoon utilizes privacy-conscious, cookieless analytics to understand aggregated traffic patterns (e.g. which articles are most read) without fingerprinting or tracking individual users across the web. If you wish to disable local storage, you can adjust your browser settings at any time without losing the ability to read our content.

4. Your Legal Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, you possess the right to:

  • Request a copy of any personal data we hold associated with your email.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your email address from our subscriber records.
  • Unsubscribe immediately via the one-click unsubscribe link embedded in every email dispatch.

5. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@thehorizoon.com
